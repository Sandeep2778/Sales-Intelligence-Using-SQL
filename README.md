## SALES INTELLIGENCE USING SQL
Retail Sales Data Analysis using MySQL to generate business insights on sales, profit, customer performance, product performance, regional sales, and monthly trends.


### 1. Project Overview

The Sales Intelligence Using SQL project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using MySQL to extract meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

The project involves exploring sales transactions, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, regional sales distribution, and monthly revenue trends.

By using SQL queries and different analytical techniques, this project transforms raw sales data into valuable business information that helps organizations understand their sales performance, identify profitable products, evaluate customer contributions, and discover opportunities for business growth.


### 2. Tools & Technologies Used
MySQL: For database management and SQL-based data analysis.
MySQL Workbench: For writing, executing, and managing SQL queries.
SQL (Structured Query Language): For data extraction, filtering, aggregation, and analysis.

### 3. Dataset Description

The project uses a retail sales dataset containing 100 sales records.

**Dataset Details:**

Total Sales Records: 100
Total Customers: 20
Product Categories: Electronics and Furniture
Sales Regions: North, South, East, and West
Data Includes: Customer details, product information, sales, quantity, profit, region, and order dates.

The dataset provides an opportunity to analyze different aspects of retail business performance.

### 4. SQL Concepts & Techniques Used

The project demonstrates practical knowledge of the following SQL concepts:

SELECT: Retrieve specific information from database tables.
WHERE: Filter records based on specific conditions.
GROUP BY: Group data for meaningful business summaries.
ORDER BY: Sort sales and profit results.
HAVING: Filter aggregated results.
JOIN: Combine multiple tables to analyze related business information.
Aggregate Functions: Use SUM(), COUNT(), AVG(), MIN(), and MAX() for calculations.
Date Functions: Analyze monthly and date-wise sales trends.


### 5. Business Analysis & Key Areas

The project covers several important business analysis areas:

Total Sales Analysis: Calculate overall revenue generated by the business.
Total Profit Analysis: Identify the total profit earned from sales transactions.
Region-wise Sales Analysis: Compare sales performance across North, South, East, and West regions.
Category-wise Sales Analysis: Evaluate the performance of Electronics and Furniture categories.
Customer Performance Analysis: Identify top-performing customers based on their purchase value and contribution to sales.
Product Performance Analysis: Find the best-selling and most profitable products.
Monthly Sales Analysis: Analyze monthly revenue trends and identify high- and low-performing periods.
Quantity Analysis: Understand product demand based on quantities sold.
Profitability Analysis: Identify products and categories generating higher profits.
Sales Comparison: Compare performance across different regions and product categories.
